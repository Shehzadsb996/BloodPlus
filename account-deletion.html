Delete Your BloodPlus Account

App: BloodPlus (com.shehzadch.bloodplus)

You can delete your BloodPlus account and the data linked to it at any time. Choose either option below.

Option 1: Delete inside the App

  1. Open BloodPlus and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the App, email shehzadsb435@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my BloodPlus account". Please send it from the email address used for your account so we can verify it is you. We will process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login account (email / Google Sign-In link)
  • Your profile: name, phone number, blood group, and address
  • Your donor listing, so you no longer appear in searches

What may remain

Encrypted backups held by our service provider (Google Firebase) may persist for a short period before they are permanently erased. We do not keep any other copy of your data.

Just want to hide your details?

You do not have to delete your account to stop being listed. In the App you can switch off your donor listing at any time.

See also our Privacy Policy.
