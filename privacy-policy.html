BloodPlus – Privacy Policy

App name: BloodPlus  |  Package: com.shehzadch.bloodplus  |  Effective date: November 9, 2026

BloodPlus ("the App", "we", "us") is a free, non-profit community platform that helps people find blood donors. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Email address (and name/profile details provided by Google Sign-In, if you use it)To create your account and let you log in.
Phone numberSo people who need blood can contact donors.

We do not collect your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health records. We do not collect payment information because the App is completely free.

3. How we use your information

Your information is used only for blood donation purposes: to run your account and to let people searching for a blood donor find and contact you. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it for marketing.

4. You control whether you are listed

Joining BloodPlus is optional and open to everyone. You decide whether your donor details are listed publicly. If you choose to be listed, other users of the App can see your blood group, area, and contact details so they can reach you. If you choose not to be listed, your details are not shown in donor searches. You can change this choice at any time inside the App.

5. Who can see your information

  • Other app users: only the details of donors who chose to be listed.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and Google Sign-In to store data and handle login. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms: Google Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

6. Our role and what BloodPlus is not

BloodPlus is only a platform that connects people looking for blood with willing donors. We do not recommend or promote any hospital, doctor, institute, disease, or treatment, and the App does not provide medical advice. Always confirm blood group, health eligibility, and safety through a qualified medical professional or licensed blood bank before any donation or transfusion.

7. Data security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored using Google Firebase security features. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the App: go to Profile → Delete Account.
  • On the web: follow the steps on our Account Deletion page.

Deleted data is removed from our active database. Short-lived backups held by our providers may persist for a limited time before being permanently erased.

9. Your rights

You may access, correct, hide, or delete your information at any time, either in the App or by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Children's privacy

BloodPlus is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date updated above.
